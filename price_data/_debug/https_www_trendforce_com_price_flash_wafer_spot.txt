NAND Flash Wafer Contract Price Sep. 2026

Driven by weak consumer end-market demand, NAND Flash wafer prices have stagnated at high levels. Although enterprise demand remains strong due to AI, inventory continues to pile up on the consumer side, leading suppliers to manage stock through bundled sales. Buyers show extremely low willingness to chase higher prices, with only select specifications seeing slight increases while overall prices consolidate sideways. If consumer inventory digestion continues to fall short of expectations, market prices face a heightened risk of a downward pull-back.

NAND Flash Wafer Contract Price Aug. 2026

High prices of prime wafers are squeezing profits for module makers. Coupled with sluggish end-user demand, manufacturers are shifting to purchase downgraded materials to supply secondary markets and reduce costs—a move original suppliers welcome to clear inventory. The overall market is fluctuating at high levels. Aside from slight price increases in specific specs driven by supply constraints or rebound adjustments in high-capacity products, most items remain stable as buyers and sellers continue their price tug-of-war.
